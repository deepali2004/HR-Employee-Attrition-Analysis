# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Preliminary observations from the screenshot

These observations describe displayed counts only. They do not explain causation.

1. **Age group:** The 26–35 group has the largest displayed attrition count (116).
2. **Salary slab:** The under-5k slab has the largest displayed attrition count (163).
3. **Job role:** Laboratory Technicians (62) and Sales Executives (57) have the largest displayed job-role counts.
4. **Gender:** The screenshot displays 140 male and 79 female attrition cases.

## Recommendations for further analysis

- Calculate attrition rates within each age group, salary slab, job role, and gender group, not only raw counts.
- Compare each group's attrition rate with the overall rate and account for group size.
- Investigate other available variables, such as overtime, job satisfaction, work-life balance, and years in current role, if they exist in the confirmed dataset.
- Treat findings as associations, not proof that a specific factor caused an employee to leave.
- Validate proposed interventions with HR stakeholders before making operational decisions.
